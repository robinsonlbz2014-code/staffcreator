A SMALL GUIDE

From a thought to a score.

  1. Choose a voice. Add instruments on the left. Each part has its own notes; all parts start together.
  2. Write a melody. Choose a duration and octave, then tap C–B or type A–G. “In key” follows the selected key signature. Use Rest or R for silence. Triplet ×3 adds three copies of the last pitch, which you can select and change.
  3. Shape a note. Click a printed note, then change its pitch, duration, dynamics, or articulation. Delete removes it. Triplet duration edits and deletion affect the whole group. Use Insert before/after to add between notes, or “Finish editing” to add at the end again.
  4. Add harmony. Turn on “Build a chord,” choose up to eight pitches (across octaves), and add the chord.
  5. Arrange & listen. Instrument pitch shows written parts; Concert pitch shows the sounding notes. Changing an instrument preserves the sounding pitches. Mute parts to audition an arrangement.
  6. Take it with you. Save a project backup, print or save a PDF, or export MusicXML, MIDI, and SVG.

Notes crossing barlines split into tied notes automatically. Ties only connect identical consecutive pitches. Slurs and fermatas are notation marks; fermatas do not change playback timing. Common dynamics, accents, staccato and tenuto affect playback.

Playback uses online General MIDI instrument samples when available and a built-in synthesizer otherwise. Timbres are previews. Instrument ranges and physical playability are not enforced. For piano grand staff, add both the right-hand and left-hand parts. Drums, guitar tablature, repeats and MusicXML import are not supported.
